# Analytical scope and business questions

## 1. Acquisition, onsite behavior, and funnel

- Which traffic sources drive session volume, purchase conversion, and acquisition-attributed value?
- Where do sessions exit between product view, cart, and purchase?
- Which channels, browsers, countries, and session-depth groups show high cart abandonment?
- Is channel concentration creating growth risk?
- How do conversion and abandonment change over complete months?

## 2. Revenue and growth

- How do gross sales, net sales, profit, customers, orders, AOV, and items per order move over time?
- Is growth driven by customer/order volume or basket value?
- Which categories, brands, countries, and acquisition sources combine scale, margin, and low leakage?
- How much value is cancelled or returned?
- Which trends are valid after excluding the incomplete latest month?

## 3. Customers and lifecycle

- How large is the repeat-customer base?
- Which acquisition cohorts retain purchase activity?
- Which RFM segments should receive retention, reactivation, or VIP treatment?
- How do value and return behavior differ by country, age band, gender, and source?

## 4. Product and merchandising

- Which categories and brands lead sales, profit, units, and margin?
- Which high-volume products underperform on revenue or profit?
- Which product groups show elevated observed return rate?
- Which category pairs have meaningful basket association for cross-sell?

## 5. Operations, delivery, returns, and inventory

- How long are order-to-ship, ship-to-delivery, and end-to-end stages?
- Which distribution centers have high median or tail (P90) lead time?
- What portion of records are valid for delivery analysis?
- Which segments have high return or cancellation rates?
- Which inventory groups have low sell-through, long time-to-sell, or aged unsold value?

## Explicitly unsupported claims

- Product-level cart abandonment: cart events do not contain cart lines or order IDs.
- Causal channel effectiveness: no marketing spend or randomized allocation is present.
- Historical A/B lift: no assignment/exposure table exists.
- External delivery competitiveness: no verified market benchmark is included.
